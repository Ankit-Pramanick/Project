skillshift --help

01. Drop a PDF/DOCX resume on the left panel.

02. Pick a question count (10 / 15 / 20), then Generate.

03. Gemini extracts skills and builds a timed MCQ + case assessment (~1 min).

04. Submit — scoring is server-side; the answer key never leaves the server.

05. copy / export the result, or clear to start over.
